AIR2GREEN
Connecting…

Data, live: PM2.5, PM10, dust and carbon monoxide from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) through the free Open-Meteo air-quality API; wind and mixing height from Open-Meteo weather forecasts; schools, hospitals, roads and parks from OpenStreetMap contributors via the Overpass API. Country shapes: Natural Earth. Places: GeoNames.

CAMS values are model estimates for a ~10–45 km grid cell, refreshed through the day, not readings from a single monitor; a street next to heavy traffic can be worse. Air quality bands are defined for 24-hour averages and are applied here to hourly values as a guide. Exposure and tree figures are estimates with their assumptions shown. This is not medical advice.
